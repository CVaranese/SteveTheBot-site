Positions and states are the simulator's own, drawn as blocks: there are no character animations. The block shows where the character is, which way it faces (the notch) and a red outline while it is in hitstun. The line under the name is the animation it is in. The dashed box is roughly where a character is lost. The same samples are used for every model, so two pages can be compared.
