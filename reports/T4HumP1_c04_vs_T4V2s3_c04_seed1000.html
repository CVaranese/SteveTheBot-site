How to read the rows

Doing: what the character is doing, one coloured block per animation, named when there is room (zoom in to read the names).

Moves: a triangle where an attack, grab or special move starts: green hit, red hit nothing, grey blocked by a shield.

Could act: every decision (30 a second) was tried again with all 54 inputs. Grey: locked, no input could change anything in the next half second (in the middle of a move, in hitstun, landing). Blue: an input could only move it (drift, walk speed) but not make it do something else. Green: free, an input could start something different.

Input did: what the input the bot really chose did. Full dark bar: it changed what the character does. Half blue bar: it only moved it. Empty: the game ignored it (same result as letting go of the controller).

Critic: the network's own guess at how the rest of the game goes for player 1 (thick, about +1 per stock it expects to take, -1 per stock it expects to lose), and what then really happened (thin).

How this was measured
Combos in "Jump to a moment" are found from the damage alone (hits less than two seconds apart), so they can differ a little from the counts in the tables.
