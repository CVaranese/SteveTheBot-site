CPU level 9: Melee's own hardest computer player, in Slippi Dolphin (20 to 100 games). Frozen T21L6Cs4 and scripted bot level 9: 100 games each in the simulator. Scenarios: the same saved positions for every model, 2 repeats. One number can move by about 5 points by chance alone. Models that act every frame with a reaction delay (rows 12 on) score near zero against undelayed models at the usual training length. In the scenarios they start each position with nothing in their delay queue, so they stand still for the first 11 to 15 frames: an attack already 8 or 9 frames away (Shielding) always lands, and their scenario numbers are not directly comparable with the rows above them.

The scenario columns

Edgeguard
Opponent offstage: share of starts where the model takes the stock.
Recovery
Model offstage: share of starts where it gets back and can act.
Follow-up
After landing a hit: share with at least one more hit.
Whiff punish
Opponent missed a move nearby: share the model punishes.
First hit
From neutral: share where the model lands the first hit.
Shielding
A move coming at the model: share where it is not hit.
