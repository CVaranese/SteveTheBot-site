Human start, two more seeds

15 Nov 2026 · T-46 · Replays

Abstract

From the imitation model, PPO is level with plain PPO on the pool and ahead against the level 9 CPU.

On the frozen pool the two starts are level (26.5 and 30.5, not separated). Against the level 9 CPU the human start won 15 of 100 games against 13 of 100; with the earlier seeds, 36 of 150 against 21 of 200.

Question and hypothesis

An earlier experiment started PPO from a model that had first been trained to copy human replay games (the imitation model), and those runs scored above runs started from newly initialised weights. That result rested on two runs. This experiment repeats the comparison with two more runs of each start at the settings in use on the day, to see whether the advantage holds.

Written in the experiment plan on 15 Nov 2026, before the runs:

Noise: same-setting seeds differ by about 10 wins of 100, so 2-seed differences under about 20 are "not separated" (T-41).

Human start (T46Hums3-4 vs T10W128s1-2): guess, ahead on pool 2 and clearly ahead on the level 9 CPU (T4HumP1 21 of 50 against 4 and 4 for plain). Ahead on both with all 4 seeds: the human start is settled as the better start (T-46 closes with that). Not ahead: imitation's advantage was the two lucky seeds; T-46 reports it.

"Pool 2" is the fixed set of four saved opponents described under Setup, and "the level 9 CPU" is the game's own computer player. T4HumP1 is one of the two earlier runs started from the imitation model. The combined counts in the last sentence of the abstract add those earlier runs to the runs of this experiment; the tables below hold this experiment's runs only.

Discussion

On the frozen pool the two starts are not separated. The plain start won 30% and the human start 27%. The difference is smaller than the bar the plan set for two runs per variant, and the two runs of the human start differ from each other by more than the two variants do (the "Each run" column).

Against the level 9 computer player the human start is slightly ahead, within chance. It won 15 of 100 games, the plain start 13 of 100 games. The intervals in the table overlap almost entirely.

The hypothesis was not confirmed on either scale. It expected the human start ahead on the pool and clearly ahead against the computer player; the starts are level on the pool, and the lead against the computer player is within chance. By the plan's own rule this experiment does not settle the human start as the better one.

Limitations

  • Two training runs per variant; differences of this size are within the spread between runs of the same settings.
  • The two variants used different seeds (21 and 22 against 23 and 24), so the runs are not paired.
  • One training length. Whether the starts differ at longer lengths was not tested here.
  • The imitation model itself was not retrained; both human-start runs begin from the same saved model.
  • The frozen pool is made of this project's own models, one of which was itself started from the imitation model.
  • The scores were measured on the copy saved at the end of each run.

Decision

Pending: the user's read. More seeds at 12 times length come with the controller batch.
