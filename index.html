Model names

What the names on these pages mean. More detail, and every other model, on the roadmap.

Parts of a name

  • T<number> at the startThe task that trained it (T21 is task T-21). Older models have no task number.
  • s<digit> or a last digitThe random seed: the same settings trained again from a different random start. Two seeds of one setting can differ a lot, so one run says little on its own.
  • Self / BotTrained by playing against itself, or against the level 3 scripted bot.
  • No checkpoint suffixTraining saves copies part-way (files named _c01, _c02, ...). Every model on this site is the copy saved at the end of its run, so the suffix is left out.

The models on this site

  • PPO8BotAs PPO8Self, but trained against the level 3 scripted bot.
  • PPO8Bot_edit_recPPO8Bot with its weights edited by hand, no training: choices made sharper, plus a hand-wired recovery circuit.
  • PPO8SelfPPO, the first new training method, with the repaired network of task T-8; trained against itself for 20 minutes.
  • SimBaseThe 2017 learner (actor-critic), moved unchanged into the simulator and trained against itself for 48,000 updates (28 minutes). The starting point everything else is compared with.
  • T21L6Cs3As T21L6Cs4, random seed 3.
  • T21L6Cs4Task T-21: self-play at twice the usual learning rate (L6: 0.0006), held constant for the whole run (C), random seed 4. The strongest model of its batch, and the opponent in the scenario tests.
  • T22Base5Task T-22: the T21L6Cs4 settings again (usual inputs), random seed 5.
  • T22Base6As T22Base5, random seed 6.
  • T22EmbSmall3Task T-22: the T21L6Cs4 settings plus an animation embedding (each animation turned into 32 learned numbers, started small), random seed 3.
  • T9PrevTask T-9 input experiment: self-play like PPO8Self, plus the bot's own previous button press as an input.
  • scripted bot level 9Not a network: a hand-written Falcon that recovers and attacks. Level 9 is its hardest setting.
