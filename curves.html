Reading the graphs

Decision
One controller input chosen by the bot. Training length is counted in decisions made by one player; a run of the usual length is 48 million.
Checkpoint
A copy of the model saved during training. It is scored later by playing games against opponents that no longer change.
Runs and seeds
The same setting trained more than once, with different random numbers. Runs of one setting differ by about 10 wins of 100, so a setting is judged by the mean of its runs.
Not on this page
Models whose checkpoints were never scored one by one, and the earliest runs, whose checkpoints were saved by the clock, not by amount of training. Their end results are in the rankings and the catalog.
