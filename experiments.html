PPO from imitation (the human line)

Does starting PPO from a network that copied human Falcon replays beat starting from scratch?

Variant (models)Frozen T4V2 (4 seeds)Scripted bot 9vs plain start
From scratch (reference, T-37 plain runs)T37Plain7, T37Plain848%47 · 500%1 · 0–
The imitation network alone, no PPO (reference)T4ImFull1%0%0%
The imitation network with frame history alone, no PPO (reference)T4ImFullH41%0%0%
From the imitation networkT4HumP1, T4HumP262%72 · 5224%40 · 9ref.
From the imitation network with frame historyT4HumH1, T4HumH247%45 · 492%3 · 134%36 · 32
From imitation, small pull back to the human policy (KL 0.1)T4HumKa1, T4HumKa238%44 · 334%5 · 224%30 · 18
From imitation, strong pull back to the human policy (KL 1.0)T4HumKb1, T4HumKb22%1 · 20%0 · 01%1 · 0
Verdict
Starting from imitation helps: mean 62 wins of 100 against frozen T4V2, against 48 from scratch at the same 2,940 updates, though the two seeds differ by 20 (72 and 52), so the gain is likely, not firm. The imitation network alone wins almost nothing (1 of 100), so the gain comes only after PPO moves away from it. The history start scores 47 and loses head to head to the plain start (34 of 100). Pulling the policy back toward the imitation network made it weaker: mean 38 with a small pull, as weak as imitation alone with a strong one. Against the level 9 in-game CPU in Dolphin (50 games): T4HumP1 won 21, the small-pull T4HumKa1 12, imitation alone 0; delayed models from scratch won 1 to 3.
Decision
PPO from imitation without a pull back is the best start found. Whether the imitation-based models look more human is for the user to watch.

First imitation runs

Can a network that copies human replays play on its own?

Variant (models)Scripted bot 9
Copy the replaysT4Im3000%
Copy the replays, input changes weighted 5 timesT4Im300W50%
Verdict
Not on its own. It learns to repeat its last input (84% accuracy, the same as "repeat the last input") and loses every game, mostly by falling off the stage without using up-B. Weighting input changes makes it predict them (24%) but does not fix recovery: the network reaches positions humans rarely show. Trained on 254 games.
Decision
The user chose the next step: imitation on all games, then PPO from that start (above).
